Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2025 • How RideX uses cookies and local storage technologies

1

Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how RideX uses cookies and related browser storage technologies to recognize your device when you visit our web application. It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your controls over them.

2

What are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit websites. They are widely used to maintain security sessions, remember your ride preferences, and optimize site response speed.

3

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required for authentication sessions (Spring Security JSESSIONID), CSRF protection tokens, and secure checkout navigation.

Functional Cookies

Remember your recent pickup locations, UI theme preferences, and filter configurations for seamless repeat bookings.

Performance Telemetry

Aggregated server metrics (Prometheus & Grafana) that help us detect slow API routes and keep page load times under 200ms.

4

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies, although certain features like one-click location memory may not function optimally without them.
