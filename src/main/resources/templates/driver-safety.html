Operational Guidelines

1 Before the Trip

  • Inspect passenger profile, verified badges, and mutual peer reviews before accepting requests.
  • Check tire pressure, fuel levels, brake fluid, and windscreen visibility.
  • Ensure smartphone is securely mounted and kept on high battery or connected to a charger.

2 During the Journey

  • All occupants must wear seatbelts at all times; obey posted highway speed limits.
  • Maintain professional courtesy; zero tolerance for alcohol or substance impairment.
  • Follow the designated GPS corridor and notify through the app if any essential route detours occur.

3 After Arrival

  • Confirm passenger drop-off at a well-lit and secure location.
  • Leave fair and constructive feedback to help keep the community accountable.
  • Check the rear seats for forgotten passenger belongings before departing.
