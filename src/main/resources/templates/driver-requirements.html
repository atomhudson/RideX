Eligibility Criteria

Ride Sharing Requirements

Transparent standards ensuring safety, reliability, and community trust for every driver offering seats on RideX.

Driver Eligibility

  • Minimum 18 years of age.
  • Valid Indian Driving License (LMV / Transport).
  • Verified mobile phone and email address.
  • Clear government ID check via DigiLocker.

Vehicle Standards

  • Valid Vehicle Registration Certificate (RC).
  • Active Motor Vehicle Insurance policy with passenger coverage.
  • Valid Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate.
  • Working air-conditioning and clean seatbelts for all seats.

Hardware & Conduct

  • GPS-enabled smartphone with active cellular data.
  • Sticking to departure schedule and verified routes.
  • Zero cancellation policy without advance notice.
  • Courteous communication with confirmed riders.
