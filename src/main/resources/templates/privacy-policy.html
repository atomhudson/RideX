Data Protection & Trust

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025 • Your trust and privacy are foundational to RideX

1

Introduction

Welcome to RideX's Privacy Policy. This document explains how we collect, use, encrypt, and safeguard your personal information when you use our carpooling platform. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data security and transparency.

2

Information We Collect

2.1 Personal & Profile Data

  • Full name, verified email address, and mobile phone number.
  • DigiLocker verified credentials (Aadhaar, Driving License, RC Book).
  • Profile photograph and community ratings.
  • Encrypted payment identifiers handled by Razorpay.

2.2 Geolocation & Route Telemetry

We collect location data solely to facilitate optimal ride matching and live passenger journey safety:

  • Pickup points, drop-off destinations, and intermediate waypoints.
  • Real-time GPS coordinates transmitted during active rides for live tracking.
  • Estimated travel duration and road corridor telemetry.
3

How We Use Your Information

  • Match co-travelers traveling along compatible routes with minimal detours.
  • Facilitate instant boarding verification via unique ride OTP codes.
  • Process secure seat reimbursement and payment split settlements.
  • Deliver mission-critical trip alerts, schedule adjustments, and live driver updates.
  • Maintain trust, prevent duplicate accounts, and comply with legal requirements.
4

Information Sharing & Privacy Safeguards

We never sell your personal information. Data is shared exclusively under limited, necessary circumstances:

  • Matched Co-Travelers: First name, profile photo, vehicle details, and pickup pin during an active booking.
  • Service Partners: Razorpay for PCI-compliant payments and DigiLocker for government verification.
  • Emergency & Law Enforcement: Strictly when mandated by applicable law or in acute emergency safety situations.
5

Data Security & Encryption

We implement enterprise-grade security controls to protect your data around the clock:

  • End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption across all network transmissions.
  • Argon2 / BCrypt cryptographic hashing for passwords and sensitive tokens.
  • Strict role-based access controls (RBAC) preventing unauthorized database access.
6

Your Rights & Data Control

  • Request a full export copy of your stored account data.
  • Correct or update inaccurate personal profile records at any time.
  • Delete your account and revoke active vehicle registration data.
  • Revoke location tracking permissions directly via your browser or mobile OS.
